# Sampler demonstration sound

`demo.wav` is an original generated waveform: two seconds of mono, 16-bit PCM
at 22,050 Hz. Four half-second tones use 220, 330, 440 and 550 Hz, with a quiet
second harmonic and short fades. It contains no third-party recording.

Replace the path in `18_sampler.py` with your own PCM WAV to sample a voice,
an instrument or a field recording.
